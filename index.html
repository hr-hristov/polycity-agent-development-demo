Agent adds a new development from a developer's words

A property developer asks Polycity's Agent for about 650 homes on a plot by Tartu mnt in Tallinn. Agent fills in the New development change and adds it once the developer says yes, and the developer lays a courtyard block on the plot.

In the film

  1. 0:00The developer signs in and opens a new, empty scenario on an ordinary weekday in Tallinn.
  2. 0:30They ask Agent for about 650 homes and the shops' 50 jobs on a plot by Tartu mnt, giving the plot's corners from the site plan.
  3. 0:50Agent fills in the New development change on that plot. Nothing changes until the developer presses Add to draft.
  4. 1:30The developer opens the change. Each building form shows the homes it would give on this plot.
  5. 1:40They pick Courtyard: 7 to 10 floors with a 12-floor corner give 636 homes, with 1,200 m² of shops and 300 parking spaces.
  6. 1:50Saved, the courtyard block stands on the plot in 3D, ready to run with and without it.

What Agent does not do yet

Agent places the homes and jobs on the plot. The buildings are laid out in the editor, where the developer picks a form and Polycity draws the block and counts its homes from the floors.

Filmed on 6 November 2026 on Polycity's test site, with real answers from Agent.
