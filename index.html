Agent adds a new development from a developer's words

A property developer asks Polycity's Agent for a courtyard block of 641 homes by Tartu mnt in Tallinn. Agent finds the place, fills in the New development change and adds it to the scenario once the developer says yes.

In the film

  1. 0:00The developer signs in and opens a new, empty scenario on an ordinary weekday in Tallinn.
  2. 0:30They ask Agent for the whole courtyard block: 641 homes, 6 to 8 floors, 1,200 m² of shops, 300 underground parking spaces and a mix of homes.
  3. 1:40Agent will not make up the outline of each building. It asks for them and leaves the scenario as it was.
  4. 2:20Asked for the homes and the shops' 50 jobs on the ground around the junction, Agent proposes the New development change.
  5. 2:50The developer presses Add to draft, and the change is in the scenario.
  6. 3:10Back in the editor, the development is one of the scenario's changes, ready to run.

What Agent does not do yet

The buildings themselves, such as the courtyard's outline and floors, are drawn in the editor. Agent places the homes and jobs on the plot and leaves the building outlines to the developer.

Filmed on 6 November 2026 on Polycity's test site, with real answers from Agent.
